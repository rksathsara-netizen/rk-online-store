Frequently asked questions

How do I place an order?+
Simply tap the WhatsApp button anywhere on this site, tell us what you're looking for, and our team will confirm availability, price and delivery details with you directly.
Do you deliver island-wide?+
Yes, we deliver across Sri Lanka. Delivery time varies by area — we'll give you an estimate as soon as your order is confirmed.
What payment methods do you accept?+
Cash on Delivery is available in most areas, and we also accept bank transfers. Payment details are confirmed on WhatsApp before dispatch.
Can I return or exchange an item?+
Yes — if there's an issue with your order, message us within 24 hours of delivery and we'll arrange a replacement or refund.
How do I know if an item is in stock?+
Just ask us on WhatsApp with the item name — we'll confirm current stock and pricing right away.
Is RK Online Store.lk a registered local business?+
Yes, we're based in Piliyandala and operate as a local Sri Lankan online store, reachable directly via WhatsApp and email for any questions.
