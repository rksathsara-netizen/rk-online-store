About RK Online Store.lk

A Piliyandala-based online store on a simple mission: make quality shopping easy, honest and affordable for every Sri Lankan household.

Our story

Started local, built for everyone

RK Online Store.lk started with a simple idea — that online shopping in Sri Lanka should feel personal, not distant. We're based right here in Piliyandala, and every order that comes through WhatsApp is handled by real people who care about getting it right.

We're not trying to be the biggest store — we're trying to be the one you trust. That means honest pricing, genuine products, and a team that actually replies when you message.

As we grow, our promise stays the same: shop more, pay less, live better.
